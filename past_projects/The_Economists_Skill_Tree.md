# **🗺️ The Economist's Skill Tree: A Comprehensive Roadmap (v2.0)**

Status: Undergrad Level 1  
Current Objective: Choose a Specialization Class  
Difficulty: Hard (Hidden Prerequisites)

## **🛑 Phase 1: The "Tutorial" (The Base Stats)**

*Every economist, regardless of specialization, must master these. If you are weak here, you cannot advance to a specialized class.*

### **1\. The Trinity (Theory)**

* **Microeconomics:** Optimization, Supply/Demand, Consumer Theory.  
* **Macroeconomics:** IS-LM, AS-AD, GDP accounting, Inflation dynamics.  
* **Econometrics:** OLS (Ordinary Least Squares), Hypothesis testing, Confidence intervals.

### **2\. The Tool (Math)**

* **Calculus I & II:** Derivatives and Integrals (Language of change).  
* **Linear Algebra:** Matrices (Language of data). *Crucial for econometrics.*  
* **Probability & Stats:** Distributions, Expectations, Variance.

### **3\. The Weapon (Software)**

* **Excel:** Lookups, Pivot Tables, basic charts. (Do not underestimate this; the world runs on Excel).

## **⚔️ Phase 2: Choose Your Class (The Core Paths)**

*These are the four traditional pillars of the profession.*

### **🧙‍♂️ Class A: The Academic Archmage (The PhD Track)**

*The path of pure intellect. High barrier to entry, long grind, high prestige.*

* **Goal:** Professor, Central Bank Researcher, IMF/World Bank Research Dept.  
* **The "Hidden" Requirement:** It’s not about Economics; it’s about **Real Analysis**.

#### **🌳 The Skill Tree**

1. **Math (Must Max Out):**  
   * **Calculus III (Multivariate):** Absolute minimum.  
   * **Real Analysis:** The "Gatekeeper" (Proofs, Epsilon-Delta). If you can't pass this, top PhDs are closed.  
   * **Topology / Measure Theory:** For advanced theory (Optional but "God Tier").  
2. **Coding:**  
   * **LaTeX:** You cannot write papers in Word. Learn BibTeX.  
   * **Matlab / Julia:** For solving heavy macro models.  
   * **Stata/R:** For empirical work (Stata is still dominant in older academia; R in newer).  
3. **Special Abilities:**  
   * **Research Design:** Finding a gap in literature.  
   * **Academic Writing:** Formal, precise, passive voice.

### **🥷 Class B: The Tech Rogue (Data Science & Tech Econ)**

*The path of agility and high salary. Solves business problems using econ logic.*

* **Goal:** Amazon Economist, Uber Pricing Team, Data Scientist at Spotify/Netflix.  
* **The "Hidden" Requirement:** It’s not about Theory; it’s about **Coding & Prediction**.

#### **🌳 The Skill Tree**

1. **Coding (Must Max Out):**  
   * **SQL:** The most important skill never taught in Econ. You need to pull your own data.  
   * **Python:** The industry standard. Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-Learn.  
2. **Econometrics (The "Causal" Edge):**  
   * **A/B Testing:** The bread and butter of tech.  
   * **Causal Inference:** Difference-in-Differences (DiD), IV, RDD. *This is your superpower over normal Data Scientists.*  
   * **Machine Learning:** Random Forests, Gradient Boosting (XGBoost).  
3. **Special Abilities:**  
   * **Data Cleaning:** 80% of the job is fixing messy data.  
   * **Visualization:** Tableau, PowerBI, or D3.js.

### **🛡️ Class C: The Policy Paladin (Public Sector & NGOs)**

*The path of impact. Translating numbers into human welfare.*

* **Goal:** Government Ministry, UN, Think Tanks, NGOs, Central Bank (Policy arm).  
* **The "Hidden" Requirement:** It’s not about Complexity; it’s about **Communication**.

#### **🌳 The Skill Tree**

1. **Tools:**  
   * **Stata:** Still the king of policy/government analysis.  
   * **Excel:** Advanced modeling (Financial models, Cost-Benefit Analysis).  
2. **Analysis:**  
   * **Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA):** Net Present Value, Social Discount Rates.  
   * **Policy Evaluation:** Did this law actually work? (Basic Impact Evaluation).  
3. **Special Abilities:**  
   * **Brief Writing:** Summarizing a 50-page paper into a 1-page memo for a Minister.  
   * **Stakeholder Management:** Navigating politics and bureaucracy.

### **💰 Class D: The Finance Merchant (Banking & Consulting)**

*The path of markets. High stress, high reward, fast-paced.*

* **Goal:** Investment Banking, Equity Research, Macro Strategy, Management Consulting (McKinsey/Bain).  
* **The "Hidden" Requirement:** Speed and **Market Awareness**.

#### **🌳 The Skill Tree**

1. **Tools:**  
   * **Excel (God Mode):** Shortcuts, VBA macros, Financial Modeling (DCF, LBO).  
   * **Bloomberg Terminal:** Navigating market data.  
2. **Analysis:**  
   * **Time Series Econometrics:** ARIMA, GARCH, Vector Autoregression (VAR).  
   * **Accounting:** Reading balance sheets (Econ students often fail this).  
3. **Special Abilities:**  
   * **Storytelling:** "Selling" a stock/strategy based on a macro trend.  
   * **Commercial Awareness:** Knowing what the Fed did yesterday and why the market moved.

## **🚀 Phase 3: The "Expansion Pack" Classes (Niche & Modern)**

*These are distinct, highly specialized paths that often get overlooked.*

### **⚖️ Class E: The Litigation Mercenary (Economic Consulting)**

*The path of high-stakes argument. distinct from management consulting.*

* **Goal:** Analysis Group, NERA, Cornerstone Research, CRA.  
* **The Job:** Calculating damages for billion-dollar lawsuits (Apple vs Samsung) or Antitrust (Mergers).  
* **The "Hidden" Requirement:** Extreme attention to detail (zero errors allowed) \+ Legal Frameworks.

#### **🌳 The Skill Tree**

1. **Tools:**  
   * **SAS / Stata:** These firms often use legacy codebases because they are "court defensible."  
   * **Excel:** For damage models.  
2. **Analysis:**  
   * **Industrial Organization (IO):** Understanding market concentration (HHI Index), monopoly power.  
   * **Applied Econometrics:** Very rigorous panel data work.  
3. **Special Abilities:**  
   * **Audit Trails:** Documenting every single step of code so it holds up in court.

### **🧠 Class F: The Behavioral Architect (UX & Product)**

*The path of psychology. Where Economics meets Product Design.*

* **Goal:** "Nudge Units" (Government), Tech Product Design, Marketing Science, User Research.  
* **The "Hidden" Requirement:** Experimental Design (Surveys/Lab).

#### **🌳 The Skill Tree**

1. **Tools:**  
   * **Survey Tools:** Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, SurveyCTO.  
   * **R / Python:** For analyzing experiment data.  
2. **Analysis:**  
   * **Experimental Design:** RCTs (Randomized Control Trials), Factorial Designs.  
   * **Qualitative Methods:** Interviews, focus groups (rarely taught in Econ).  
3. **Special Abilities:**  
   * **Psychology:** Kahneman/Tversky (Biases, Heuristics, Loss Aversion).  
   * **User Experience (UX):** Understanding user flows.

### **⛓️ Class G: The Cryptomancer (Tokenomics & Web3)**

*The path of digital frontiers. High risk, high innovation.*

* **Goal:** Token Economist for DAOs, DeFi Protocols, Crypto VC.  
* **The "Hidden" Requirement:** Game Theory & Mechanism Design.

#### **🌳 The Skill Tree**

1. **Tools:**  
   * **Python:** For simulation.  
   * **Solidity:** Reading smart contracts (basic literacy).  
   * **Machinations.io:** A specific tool for game economy modeling.  
2. **Analysis:**  
   * **Mechanism Design:** Designing incentives so people don't cheat the system.  
   * **Monetary Theory:** Defining inflation schedules for new currencies.  
   * **Agent-Based Modeling (ABM):** Simulating how thousands of agents interact.  
3. **Special Abilities:**  
   * **Whitepaper Writing:** Technical documentation of economic logic.  
   * **Community Management:** Explaining "why price go down" to angry users.

### **🌐 Websites & Communities**

* **AEAWeb (JOE):** The standard job board for Ph.D.s.  
* **\#EconTwitter (X):** Where the actual debates happen (often toxic, but informative).  
* **Marginal Revolution:** The daily blog every economist reads.  
* **NBER:** The preprint server where papers appear 2 years before publication.

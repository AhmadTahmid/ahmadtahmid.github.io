The Economist's Skill Tree

A rough map of the knowledge, tools and judgment that different economic practices draw upon.

Why a forest?

A video-game skill tree was the original provocation. The metaphor survived because careers share deep foundations while branching into different forms of practice. It is illustrative—not a curriculum, ranking or claim to completeness.

Reading the map

Prerequisite means a conceptual foundation. Working command means a capability that regularly produces the work. Adjacent advantage means useful leverage rather than a barrier to entry. Blank space does not mean useless.

Real jobs are messier than diagrams. Institutions, seniority, teams and research questions change the mix; software changes faster than the underlying craft.
